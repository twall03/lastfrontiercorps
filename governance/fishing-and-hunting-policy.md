# Policy on Fishing and Hunting During Trips

**Last Frontier Corps**
**Status: DRAFT. Not in effect until adopted by the Board of Directors.**

## 1. Purpose

Last Frontier Corps runs wilderness trips to strengthen the mental health of
veterans. The trips are not guided fishing or hunting trips. This policy records
how fishing and hunting are handled when they happen during a trip, so that
participants, volunteers, funders and regulators can see that Last Frontier Corps
does not provide guide services.

## 2. Policy

1. **Trips are wilderness trips.** No trip is planned, advertised or described as
   a fishing trip or a hunting trip. Fishing or hunting may take place during a
   trip as a recreational group activity when the place and season allow. It is
   never the purpose of a trip and is never promised to a participant.

2. **No guide services.** Last Frontier Corps does not provide sport fishing guide
   services or big game guide or outfitter services. No director, officer,
   employee, contractor or volunteer acts as a fishing or hunting guide on behalf
   of Last Frontier Corps.

3. **No compensation for assisting.** No person receives payment, wages or any
   other benefit from Last Frontier Corps, from a participant, or from anyone
   else in return for helping a participant fish or hunt during a trip.
   Reimbursement of actual trip expenses is not compensation.

4. **No charge to participants.** Participants pay nothing for a trip, and nothing
   for any fishing or hunting that takes place during one.

5. **Participants hold their own licences.** Anyone who fishes or hunts during a
   trip must hold the State of Alaska licences, tags, stamps and permits required
   for that activity, in their own name, before doing so. Each person is
   responsible for knowing and following the regulations for the area and season,
   including bag and possession limits.

6. **Fish and game belong to the person who takes them.** Last Frontier Corps does
   not sell, barter or take ownership of any fish or game.

7. **Paid trip leaders.** If Last Frontier Corps ever pays a person to lead or
   staff trips, that pay is for trip leadership, logistics and safety only. A paid
   person must not accompany or direct a participant in fishing or hunting, or
   handle a participant's fish or game, unless that person holds the State of
   Alaska licence required to do so and the Board has approved the arrangement in
   writing.

8. **Licensed guides acting privately.** A director, officer or volunteer who
   holds a guide licence in their own right does not exercise that licence on
   behalf of Last Frontier Corps unless the Board has approved it in writing after
   confirming the arrangement with the relevant state agency.

9. **Safety.** Trip leaders may stop any fishing or hunting activity at any time
   for safety. Firearms are handled in line with the Last Frontier Corps safety
   plan and all applicable law.

## 3. If the facts change

If Last Frontier Corps ever wishes to offer guided fishing or hunting, to pay
someone to assist with either, or to charge for a trip, the Board must first
confirm the licensing requirements with the Alaska Department of Fish and Game
and the Alaska Big Game Commercial Services Board, and amend this policy.

## 4. Review

The Board reviews this policy once a year and whenever trip activities change.

### Notes for the Board (remove before adopting)

- This draft was prepared as a starting point and is not legal advice. Have an
  Alaska attorney, or the agencies named below, review it before relying on it.
- A written policy does not by itself decide whether an activity counts as
  guiding. What counts is what actually happens on each trip. The policy matters
  because it sets the rule everyone follows and is evidence of how trips are run.
- Before adopting, confirm with the Alaska Department of Fish and Game (sport
  fishing) and the Big Game Commercial Services Board (hunting) that a free
  nonprofit trip run this way is not guiding. The governing law is believed to be
  AS 16.40 for sport fishing guides and AS 08.54 for big game guides; confirm the
  citations. Keep a note of who you spoke to, the date and what they said.
- This policy covers guide licensing only. Land-use permits for running organized
  trips on public land are a separate requirement.
