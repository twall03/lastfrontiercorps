Our Mission

The wilderness asks for what service taught you. Then it gives something back.

Many veterans come home carrying more than their gear: post-traumatic stress, moral injury, isolation, and the loss of the team and mission that once defined every day. Too many are lost to suicide each year.

Last Frontier Corps exists to change that. We pair the challenge and stillness of Alaska's backcountry with peer support and evidence-informed wellness practices, so veterans can rediscover the things that carried them through service — a team, a mission, and trust in themselves.

Every expedition is offered at no cost to the veteran, including travel, gear, food, and guides.

Our pillars

Challenge

Real objectives in real terrain. Shared hardship builds the kind of trust that's hard to find back home.

Connection

Small cohorts of fellow veterans. People who understand without needing it explained.

Healing

Time in nature, mindfulness, and guided reflection woven into every day on the trail.

Purpose

Leadership roles, service projects, and a community that keeps going long after the trip ends.

Our Story

It started with one trip.

Jeremy Ditzler served ten years as a U.S. Army infantryman, from 2005 to 2015, before receiving an honorable medical discharge. Like many veterans, he came home to a life that looked nothing like the one he'd left.

Then he took a trip into the wilderness. Out there he found new perspectives — and it changed the trajectory of his life. He went on to build that life in Alaska, where he works today as a fishing and hunting guide.

Jeremy founded Last Frontier Corps to offer other veterans the same chance: time in wild country, alongside people who understand, to see what comes next a little differently.

Programs

Expeditions built for the seasons of the Last Frontier.

Each expedition is led by experienced wilderness guides and supported by veteran peer mentors. No prior outdoor experience is required — only the willingness to show up.

Summer · 7 days

Backcountry Trek

Multi-day backpacking through alpine valleys and across glacial rivers. Navigate as a team, camp under the midnight sun.

Summer · 5 days

River & Fly Fishing

Float remote rivers during the salmon runs. Patient, focused, and quiet — a different kind of mission.

Late summer · 6 days

Sea Kayak Expedition

Paddle coastal fjords alongside tidewater glaciers, whales, and sea otters. Every crossing depends on the person beside you.

Winter · 5 days

Aurora Winter Retreat

Cabin-based snowshoeing, cold-weather skills, and long nights under the northern lights. Rest and reflection at the heart of winter.

Families & caregivers

Healing doesn't happen alone. We're building programs for spouses, Gold Star families, and caregivers so the people who stand beside our veterans can find support too.

Our Approach

The expedition is only the middle of the journey.

  1. 01

    Apply

    Share a little about your service and what you're looking for. A veteran on our team will reach out personally.

  2. 02

    Prepare

    Pre-trip calls with your cohort and guides, a gear kit shipped to your door, and a training plan matched to your fitness.

  3. 03

    Expedition

    Days of challenge in the wild, with structured peer circles, mindfulness practice, and time to simply be still.

  4. 04

    Carry It Home

    Ongoing peer check-ins, alumni gatherings, and warm connections to clinical care and VA resources when you want them.

Last Frontier Corps programs complement — but do not replace — professional mental health treatment. We work alongside clinicians and help connect participants to care.

FAQ

Questions we hear often.

Does it cost anything to attend?

No. Expeditions are free for veterans, including travel within our program area, gear, meals, and guides. We're funded by donors, grants, and partners.

Do I need wilderness experience or to be in great shape?

No. We offer expeditions at a range of physical intensities and will help you choose one that fits. Guides teach every skill you'll need.

Is this therapy?

Our expeditions are therapeutic, but they aren't a substitute for clinical treatment. We build in peer support and wellness practices and can connect you with licensed providers and VA resources.

Who is eligible?

U.S. military veterans of any era and branch. Discharge status is not a barrier to applying. We are also developing programs for families and caregivers.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Last Frontier Corps is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. Donations are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law, and you'll receive a receipt for your records.
